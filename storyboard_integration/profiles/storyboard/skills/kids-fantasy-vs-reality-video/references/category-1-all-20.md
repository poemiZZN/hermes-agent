# Category 1: Fantasy vs Reality — 20 Storylines

All use Minecraft blocky voxel style, 9:16, and American English. Use one 30s prompt for wan3.0; split the complete arc into two separately generated 15s prompts for MiniMax H3.
6-beat structure: entrance → climax → transition → reality → flashback → split-screen + warm ending.

---

## 1.1 — Rocking Horse Knight
**Fantasy**: Knight in iron armor slaying Ender Dragon on dark battlefield
**Reality**: Toddler screaming on plastic rocking horse, carrot-on-a-stick as sword
**Props**: Cardboard armor, rocking horse
**Punchline**: "Same energy."
**Ending**: Mom kisses forehead → pixel heart floats up
