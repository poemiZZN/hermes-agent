# Five Core Storylines — Full Scene Descriptions

All storylines follow the same 6-beat structure:
1. Fantasy entrance (0-Xs)
2. Fantasy climax (X-Ys)
3. Visual transition (Y-Zs)
4. Reality silly scene (Z-Ws)
5. 0.5s flashback (W-Vs)

## 1. Rocking Horse Knight (Age 3-5)

**Fantasy identity**: Blocky knight in pixel iron armor, riding a black cube warhorse
**Reality prop**: Faded blue plastic rocking horse, tinfoil cardboard armor, carrot-on-a-stick
**Punchline**: "Same energy."
**Warm ending**: Mom kisses child's forehead → pixel heart floats up → child grins showing missing tooth

**Fantasy scene**: Dark stormy medieval wasteland, burning castle in distance, 8-bit lightning. Knight draws diamond sword, horse rears. Giant Ender Dragon swoops from cliffs breathing purple pixel fire. Knight charges, leaps off horse in slow motion, strikes dragon's chest — golden XP orbs burst, dragon explodes into loot drops. Knight lands in triumphant kneel on blocky grass.

**Reality scene**: TNT explosion transition. Cube toddler with huge square head, wearing crude cardboard armor, riding faded blue rocking horse. Face distorted — mouth taking up half the face, pixel drool spraying, bright red cheeks. Screaming wildly, waving carrot-on-a-stick at nothing. Floor covered in LEGO bricks and snacks. Mom on sofa with coffee mug, deadpan (= =) expression. Kiss ending.

**Music**: Fantasy — 8-bit epic orchestral; Reality — rocking horse squeaks, screaming, cartoon spring sounds; Ending — single warm piano note.

## 2. Cardboard Box Astronaut (Age 4-5)

**Fantasy identity**: Child astronaut in white pixel spacesuit, glass-visor cube helmet
**Reality prop**: Crumpled cardboard box labeled FRAGILE, silver salad bowl as helmet, crayon dashboard
**Punchline**: "Still exploring."
**Warm ending**: Dad sticks golden pixel star sticker on box → child's eyes turn into glowing white squares

**Fantasy scene**: Redstone rocket cockpit, child presses glowing buttons, dashboard lights up. Rocket launches from blocky launchpad with blue pixel flames. Soars through purple-blue pixel nebula, Saturn with pixelated rings visible. Child dodges meteor with sharp turn, gives confident thumbs-up. Shooting star streaks across background.

**Reality scene**: Rocket dissolves into pixel particles. Cube child in bathrobe, salad bowl helmet, inside FRAGILE box. Twists violently making "vroom—whoosh—" engine sounds, spit spraying inside bowl. Scoots box across carpet. Dog on sofa with floating question mark, confused. Dad filming with phone, body shaking with suppressed laughter. Star sticker ending.

**Music**: Fantasy — 8-bit sci-fi synthwave; Reality — cardboard scooting, engine sounds, dog whimper; Ending — warm synthesizer pad chord.

## 3. Towel-Cape Superhero (Age 3-4)

**Fantasy identity**: Towering cube superhero in red pixel cape, massive dark gray/blue body
**Reality prop**: Diaper, crooked Spider-Man mask, pink bunny bath towel cape
**Punchline**: "Not all heroes wear capes. Some wear bath towels."
**Warm ending**: Dad straightens crooked mask → child gives tight hug → giant pixel heart floats up

**Fantasy scene**: Rain-soaked neon city at night. Hero descends from sky, lands on asphalt cracking in radial pattern. Charges into burning building, rescues pixel ocelot kitten. Flies up, catches spinning helicopter with one hand, sets it down. Stands on tallest skyscraper spire at sunset, cape billowing, citizens below with floating hearts and cheer particles.

**Reality scene**: Match cut — hero leaps from rooftop, toddler jumps off sofa. Diaper toddler in crooked mask, bunny towel cape. Runs wildly punching air — pixel shockwave rings appear but hit nothing. Shouts "Hi-yah! Ha! Wham!" Jumps on sofa, cape snags armrest, yanked back onto cushions — spinning pixel stars above head. Gets up, runs into coffee table, remote falls on foot. Sits pouting with inverted-V mouth, then gets up to continue. Mom and dad calmly eating dinner at table, mom casually eating vegetables. Dad straightens mask, hug ending.

**Music**: Fantasy — 8-bit heroic brass; Reality — kung-fu shouts, sofa thud, star jingle, remote clatter; Ending — warm cello string resolution.

## 4. Kitchen Mad Scientist (Age 4-6)

**Fantasy identity**: Child scientist in white lab coat and goggles
**Reality prop**: Apron, mixing bowl, cereal, ketchup, candy, shredded cheese
**Punchline**: "Science is messy."
**Warm ending**: Mom returns with bowl of cookies → wipes child's face with tissue → child grins

**Fantasy scene**: Dim underground lab, neon-green pixel glass tanks with bubbling liquid, twisted copper pipes, electric arcs cracking. Child holds glowing rainbow test tube, maniacal grin. Pours into copper cauldron — golden pixel particle mushroom cloud erupts, filling lab with light. Spinning rainbow gem floats above cauldron. Child cackles "MWAHAHAHA! I've done it!" Dashboard gauges spike, lights flicker.

**Reality scene**: Pixel smoke swirls into warm kitchen. Child in apron on stool, giant mixing bowl of gray-green paste. Dumps cereal, ketchup, candy, cheese into bowl. Stirs furiously — goop splatters everywhere. Takes big spoonful, chews twice — face transforms from anticipation to disgust, tongue out, vomit particles. Exclaims "Bleh! Yuck!" Mom peeks through door — shock → deadpan → backs out and closes door. Cookie ending.

**Music**: Fantasy — 8-bit quirky electronic; Reality — squelching paste, ketchup splat, chewing, disgusted exclamation; Ending — single warm piano note.

## 5. Hairbrush Rock Star (Age 5-7)

**Fantasy identity**: Rock star in black leather jacket and sunglasses
**Reality prop**: School uniform, socks, pink hairbrush as microphone
**Punchline**: "Rock on, little legend."
**Warm ending**: Dad puts down phone, picks up real toy guitar, hands it to child → child drops hairbrush, takes guitar, face breaks into radiant smile

**Fantasy scene**: Massive blocky stadium, tens of thousands of cube fans waving pink/purple glow sticks. Rock star rises from beneath stage, throws rock horns, crowd erupts. Grabs mic, sways with rhythm, hair flowing, belts out "WE ARE THE CHAMPIONS! YEAH!" in raspy rock voice. Crowd sings along, human wave. Rock star runs across stage, knee slide to edge. Female fan screams and faints cartoonishly. Rock star stands at highest point, arms spread, pink spotlight, fireworks explode into pixel stars.

**Reality scene**: Match cut — arms spread pose transitions to bedroom. Child in school uniform and socks on bed, facing wardrobe mirror, pink hairbrush as mic. Sings off-key "WE ARE THE CHAMPIONS! YEAH!" with total passion. Jumps off bed, tries sock slide on floor — crashes into bed frame, spinning pixel stars. Gets up immediately, continues singing, head thrashing. Dad opens door, silently pulls out phone, records, body shaking with suppressed laughter. Guitar ending.

**Music**: Fantasy — 8-bit rock electric guitar; Reality — off-key singing, hairbrush bristles, sock slide, bed frame thud; Ending — warm drum beat resolution.
