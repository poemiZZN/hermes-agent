# Category 2: Growing Up Stories — 20 Storylines

All use Minecraft blocky voxel style, 9:16, and American English. Use one 30s prompt for wan3.0; split the complete arc into two separately generated 15s prompts for MiniMax H3.
4-beat structure: setup → conflict → turning point → warm resolution.

---

## 2.1 — The Last Cookie
Two siblings, one cookie. Epic standoff. Both lunge, cookie breaks in half. Sister gives brother the bigger half. He breaks off a piece and gives it back. They eat on the floor, shoulders touching.
**Ending**: "Siblings."

## 2.2 — Dad's Secret Scar
Child scrapes knee, crying. Dad rolls up his pant leg, reveals an old scar. Child touches it gently. The scrape suddenly seems smaller. Child gets back on the bike.
**Ending**: "We all have scars."

## 2.3 — Mom's Midnight Snack
Child caught sneaking cookies at 1AM. Mom doesn't get mad. She pulls out chocolate spread, two spoons, sits down. They eat together in the dark kitchen. "Don't tell Dad."
**Ending**: "Midnight alliance."

## 2.4 — The Pillow Fort
Mom says bedtime. Siblings transform living room into a castle. Mom crawls inside. Wears the paper crown. The clock shows way past bedtime. Nobody cares.
**Ending**: "Kingdom."

## 2.5 — The Monster Check
Every night: Dad checks closet, under bed, window. Pulls out a toy dinosaur. Child hugs it. Dad ruffles hair. Turns off light. Stands in doorway. Child is already asleep.
**Ending**: "Every night. No exceptions."

## 2.6 — Learning to Tie Shoes
Dad sits on the floor with child. Two loops. "The bunny goes around the tree." Child's fingers fumble. Try again. And again. On the tenth try — the loops hold. Child looks up. Dad's eyes are wet.
**Ending**: "Bunny around the tree."

## 2.7 — The Fever Night
Child is sick. Burning up. Mom sits by the bed all night, cool cloth on forehead. Child wakes at 3AM. Mom is still there, asleep in the chair, cloth still in hand. Child reaches out. Touches mom's hand.
**Ending**: "Still here."

## 2.8 — Dad's Off-Key Lullaby
Dad can't sing. At all. But every night, he sings the same lullaby. The notes are wrong, the rhythm is off. The child is already asleep. Dad keeps singing anyway. The dog covers its ears.
**Ending**: "Every night. Same song. Still off-key."

## 2.9 — The Hand-Me-Down Jacket
Older sibling gets a new jacket. Younger sibling gets the old one. The younger one stares at the hand-me-down. Puts it on. It's too big. Sleeves cover the hands. But it smells like older sibling. The younger one hugs themselves.
**Ending**: "Still warm."

## 2.10 — Mom's Burnt Dinner
Mom tries a new recipe. The kitchen fills with smoke. The fire alarm goes off. Mom opens the window, fanning smoke. Dinner is a blackened brick on a plate. Mom looks defeated. Child takes a bite. Chews. "It's good." Takes another bite. Mom's eyes water.
**Ending**: "Best dinner ever."

## 2.11 — The Lost Teddy Bear
Child's teddy bear is missing. Searches everywhere. Under bed, in closet, behind couch. Nothing. Child is inconsolable. Dad drives to the store at 9PM. Comes back with an identical bear. "Found him." Child hugs it. Dad collapses on couch.
**Ending**: "Found him."

## 2.12 — Grandma's Secret Candy
Every visit, Grandma slips the child a piece of candy. Mom says no sugar. Grandma winks. Child hides it in a pocket. Mom pretends not to see. Grandma pretends to be innocent. The cycle continues.
**Ending**: "Grandma's pockets are magic."

## 2.13 — The Broken Vase
Child breaks mom's favorite vase. Hides the pieces. Mom finds them. Child braces for anger. Mom kneels down. "Are you okay? Did you cut yourself?" Checks the child's hands. The vase is forgotten. The child is not.
**Ending**: "It's just a vase."

## 2.14 — Dad's First Braid
Mom is away. Dad has to do the child's hair. Fumbles with the brush. YouTube tutorial playing. Three attempts. The braid is lopsided, uneven, falling apart. The child looks in the mirror. "It's perfect." Dad exhales.
**Ending**: "Dad's first braid."

## 2.15 — The Empty Bleachers
Child scores the winning goal. Looks up at the bleachers. Dad is on a work call, facing away, missed it. Child's face falls. After the game, Dad is waiting by the car. "Tell me about the goal." Dad listened to the whole thing on the radio.
**Ending**: "He was listening."

## 2.16 — The Nightmare Aftermath
Child wakes from a nightmare. Crying. Mom comes running. Child describes the monster. Mom listens seriously. Then: "I know that monster. He's afraid of lullabies." Mom sings. The monster doesn't stand a chance.
**Ending**: "The monster is afraid of lullabies."

## 2.17 — The School Play
Child has one line in the school play. One line. Forgets it. Stands frozen on stage. Complete silence. The whole audience is waiting. Then from the back row — Dad's voice, whispering the line. Child says it. The play continues. Dad is still mouthing the words.
**Ending**: "He knew every line."

## 2.18 — The Tooth Fairy Fail
Child loses a tooth. Puts it under the pillow. Wakes up. The tooth is still there. No money. Child's face falls. Mom "finds" the money under the other pillow. "The tooth fairy must have gotten confused." The child knows. The mom knows the child knows. Neither says anything.
**Ending**: "The tooth fairy gets confused sometimes."

## 2.19 — Mom's Old Photos
Child finds a box of mom's old photos. Mom as a teenager. With weird hair. Weird clothes. Mom tries to grab the box. Child runs away laughing. They end up on the couch, looking through photos together. Mom explains each one. Her voice is different. Softer.
**Ending**: "Mom was cool once."

## 2.20 — The Last Day of Summer
Last day before school starts. Child and dad at the beach. They don't talk about tomorrow. They build a sandcastle. The tide comes in. The castle washes away. Child looks at the disappearing castle. Dad puts a hand on the child's shoulder. Neither says anything. The sun sets.
**Ending**: "See you next summer."
