# Category 3: First Time vs Master — 20 Storylines

All use Minecraft blocky voxel style, 9:16, and American English.
Use terrified beginner (0-10s) → competent routine (10-20s) → confident master (20-30s). wan3.0 uses one 30s prompt. MiniMax H3 uses two separately generated 15s prompts: Segment 1 covers failure and early competence; Segment 2 completes competence, mastery, and the callback.

---

## 3.1 — Riding a Bike
Stage 1: Crashes into bush, crying, scraped knee. Stage 2: Smooth riding, signals turns, stops at signs. Stage 3: No-hands wheelie, stands on seat, high-fives the same bush.
**Ending**: "From scraped knees to no hands."

## 3.2 — Cooking
Stage 1: Egg explodes on counter, shell everywhere. Stage 2: Perfect sunny-side-up, mom nods approval. Stage 3: One-handed double egg crack, omelet flip, ketchup smiley face.
**Ending**: "From egg on face to chef's kiss."

## 3.3 — Swimming
Stage 1: Falls in shallow water, floaties twisted, sputtering. Stage 2: Clean freestyle, proper flip turns, checks clock. Stage 3: Racing dive, effortless speed, victory cannonball soaks everyone.
**Ending**: "From floaties to cannonballs."

## 3.4 — Skateboarding
Stage 1: Falls immediately on tiny ramp, pillow armor. Stage 2: Smooth cruising, tiny ollie, scuffed knee pads. Stage 3: Drops big ramp, kickflips stairs, grinds rails, sparks flying.
**Ending**: "From pillow armor to kickflips."

## 3.5 — Playing Guitar
Stage 1: Picks up guitar, strums all six strings at once, sound like a dying cat. Stage 2: Plays basic chords, switches between them slowly, brow furrowed in concentration. Stage 3: Eyes closed, shredding a solo, fingers flying, head banging.
**Ending**: "From dying cat to guitar god."

## 3.6 — Drawing
Stage 1: Crayon gripped in fist, scribbles that barely resemble a person, stick figure with one giant eye. Stage 2: Drawing a recognizable dog, coloring inside the lines, tongue poking out in concentration. Stage 3: Sketching a detailed portrait, shading with a pencil, easel and beret, looking like a real artist.
**Ending**: "From stick figures to masterpieces."

## 3.7 — Making the Bed
Stage 1: Fitted sheet snaps back, child gets tangled inside, rolls off the bed. Stage 2: Sheets are flat, pillows are in place, blanket is straight, mom gives a thumbs up. Stage 3: Military corners, pillow fluffed with a karate chop, a mint on the pillow, child salutes the bed.
**Ending**: "From tangled sheets to hotel standards."

## 3.8 — Tying Shoelaces
Stage 1: Fingers fumble, laces become a knot, child pulls the wrong end, the whole thing tightens into a ball. Stage 2: Bunny ears method, slow but correct, a neat little bow, child looks proud. Stage 3: One-handed knot, double knot, triple knot, child ties shoes without looking while walking.
**Ending**: "From giant knot to one-handed bow."

## 3.9 — Blowing Bubble Gum
Stage 1: Chews furiously, tries to blow, gum shoots out of mouth, lands on the dog. Stage 2: Small bubble forms, pops on the face, child laughs. Stage 3: Bubble as big as the head, pops it with a toothpick, catches the gum and starts chewing again.
**Ending**: "From gum on the dog to bubble champion."

## 3.10 — Whistling
Stage 1: Mouth forms an O, only air comes out, a faint wheeze, face turning red from effort. Stage 2: A thin, wavering whistle, can almost hold a tune, the dog tilts its head. Stage 3: Loud taxi whistle with two fingers, calls the dog from across the park, full melody whistling.
**Ending**: "From wheeze to taxi whistle."

## 3.11 — Juggling
Stage 1: One ball in the air, panics, the other two balls drop immediately, child just stands there holding nothing. Stage 2: Three balls, a steady rhythm, focused expression, counting under breath. Stage 3: Five balls, walking while juggling, behind the back, under the leg, finishes with a bow.
**Ending**: "From zero balls to five."

## 3.12 — Doing a Cartwheel
Stage 1: Runs, puts hands down, legs flop sideways, lands in a crumpled heap. Stage 2: Wobbly cartwheel, feet land one after the other, arms shake but it counts. Stage 3: Perfect cartwheel followed by a roundoff, back handspring, sticks the landing like a gymnast.
**Ending**: "From crumpled heap to stuck landing."

## 3.13 — Using Chopsticks
Stage 1: Holds both sticks in one fist, stabs at food like a caveman, rice flies everywhere. Stage 2: Holds them correctly, picks up one piece of sushi, drops it in the soy sauce, but it was close. Stage 3: Picks up a single grain of rice, transfers food between chopsticks, uses them to cook.
**Ending**: "From caveman stab to single grain."

## 3.14 — Folding a Paper Airplane
Stage 1: Folds paper randomly, the "plane" drops straight down, doesn't even glide. Stage 2: Follows instructions, a decent glider, flies across the room, lands on the teacher's desk. Stage 3: Designs their own model, flies from the top of the stairs, loops and glides, lands perfectly in a trash can.
**Ending**: "From straight down to bullseye."

## 3.15 — Opening a Lock with a Key
Stage 1: Key won't go in, tries upside down, tries sideways, jiggles furiously, gives up. Stage 2: Key slides in smoothly, turns with a satisfying click, the door opens. Stage 3: Keys in hand, unlocks the door while carrying groceries, talking on the phone, and holding a coffee.
**Ending**: "From jiggling fury to one-handed unlock."

## 3.16 — Peeling an Orange
Stage 1: Attacks the orange with both hands, juice sprays everywhere, peel comes off in tiny pieces, fingers are sticky, orange is destroyed. Stage 2: Neat spiral peel, sections separated cleanly, a small pile of perfect wedges. Stage 3: Peels the entire orange in one long continuous strip, drops it on the table like a magic trick, eats the orange in three bites.
**Ending**: "From sticky massacre to one-strip wonder."

## 3.17 — Wrapping a Present
Stage 1: Paper is crumpled, tape is stuck to itself, scissors are lost, the present looks like a ball of trash. Stage 2: Neat corners, even tape, a simple bow, presentable enough for a birthday party. Stage 3: Perfect corners, invisible tape, elaborate ribbon, the wrapping is as good as the gift, people don't want to open it.
**Ending**: "From trash ball to too pretty to open."

## 3.18 — Flying a Kite
Stage 1: Runs with the kite dragging on the ground, string tangles around the legs, kite never leaves the ground. Stage 2: Kite is in the air, steady, child holds the string with a relaxed grip, the kite dances in the wind. Stage 3: Kite is a dot in the sky, child does tricks, loops and dives, the string is an extension of the hand.
**Ending**: "From ground drag to sky dancer."

## 3.19 — Building a Sandcastle
Stage 1: Pile of wet sand, bucket won't release, castle collapses immediately, child sits in defeat. Stage 2: Basic castle with towers, a moat, the walls hold, a seagull lands on it. Stage 3: Elaborate castle with multiple towers, a drawbridge, carved details, people stop to take photos.
**Ending**: "From collapsed pile to tourist attraction."

## 3.20 — Cutting Paper with Scissors
Stage 1: Holds scissors upside down, paper tears instead of cuts, zigzag edges, fingers through the wrong holes. Stage 2: Clean straight line, follows the dotted line, cuts out a circle almost perfectly. Stage 3: Cuts intricate snowflake patterns, freehand, no lines, unfolds to reveal a masterpiece.
**Ending**: "From torn zigzags to snowflake magic."
