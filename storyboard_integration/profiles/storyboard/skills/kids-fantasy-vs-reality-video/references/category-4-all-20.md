# Category 4: Childhood Fears — 20 Storylines

All use Minecraft blocky voxel style, 9:16, and American English. Use one 30s prompt for wan3.0; split the complete arc into two separately generated 15s prompts for MiniMax H3.
4-beat structure: setup → fear emerges → escape → safe (but ambiguous ending).

---

## 4.1 — Running Up the Stairs in the Dark
Turned off basement light. Something is climbing the stairs behind you. Faster. Faster. You slam the door, press your back against it. The doorknob... is it turning?
**Ending**: "You know it's still there."

## 4.2 — The Midnight Bathroom Run
3 AM. You have to pee. The bathroom is at the end of the dark hallway. The hallway stretches. The family photos are watching you. You run back, dive into bed, making sure no body part hangs off the edge.
**Ending**: "3:00 AM. Every night."

## 4.3 — The Closet Door
It was closed when you went to sleep. Now it's open. Just a crack. You slam it shut, wedge a chair under the doorknob. You fall asleep. The camera pans back. The chair slides backward. One inch. By itself.
**Ending**: "It was already open."

## 4.4 — The Mirror Game
Everyone at the sleepover said it works. Don't say the name three times. You say it. The mirror fogs up. Handprints appear on the inside. You run. Later, the bathroom door opens. Just a crack.
**Ending**: "It's just a game. Right?"

## 4.5 — What's Under the Bed
Your foot is hanging off the edge. Something just touched it. You pull your foot up. Scratching sounds circle the bed. You check with a flashlight. Nothing. You fall asleep. A hand slowly rises from under the bed.
**Ending**: "You checked. It wasn't there. But it is now."

## 4.6 — The Dark Window Reflection
You're in the kitchen at night. The window over the sink is a black mirror. You see your own reflection. You wave. The reflection doesn't wave back. You blink. The reflection is closer now. You turn on the light. Nothing there. You turn off the light. The reflection is right behind you.
**Ending**: "Don't look at windows at night."

## 4.7 — The Vent That Whispers
Your bedroom has a vent on the floor. At night, you hear something. A whisper. Through the vent. You press your ear to it. The whisper stops. You pull away. The whisper starts again. It knows your name.
**Ending**: "It knows your name."

## 4.8 — The Attic Door
The attic door is in the hallway ceiling. It's always closed. Always. One night, you walk past. The pull cord is swinging. The door is open. Just a crack. You run to your room. From your bed, you can see the hallway. The attic door is still open. Something is looking down through the crack.
**Ending**: "It was always closed. Always."

## 4.9 — The Shower Curtain
You're alone in the bathroom. The shower curtain is closed. You can't see behind it. You imagine something standing there. Motionless. Waiting. You pull the curtain open. Nothing. You exhale. You close the curtain. From the other side — a handprint presses against the fabric.
**Ending**: "It was waiting for you to close it."

## 4.10 — The Creaking Floorboard
The hallway floorboard creaks. Every night. At exactly the same time. You tell yourself it's the house settling. But the creak is getting closer to your door. Night by night. Inch by inch. Tonight, it's right outside.
**Ending**: "It's not the house settling."

## 4.11 — The Doll on the Shelf
Grandma's old porcelain doll sits on the shelf. It faces the wall. Every morning, you wake up and it's facing you. You turn it back to the wall. The next morning — facing you again. You put it in the closet. That night, you hear scratching. From inside the closet.
**Ending**: "It doesn't like the closet."

## 4.12 — The TV Static
You fall asleep with the TV on. You wake up at 3 AM. The TV is static. White noise. The screen is just snow. But in the static — you can see a face. It's looking at you. You turn off the TV. The screen is black. Your reflection is in the screen. The face is still there. Behind your reflection.
**Ending**: "Turn off the TV before you sleep."

## 4.13 — The Empty Swing
You walk past the playground at night. All the swings are still. Except one. It's swinging. Just slightly. Back and forth. There's no wind. You run home. You don't look back. But you know. The swing is still moving.
**Ending**: "There was no wind."

## 4.14 — The Long Hallway at School
You stayed late for a club. The hallway is empty. All the classroom doors are closed. The lights are off. The hallway seems longer than usual. The exit sign at the end glows red. You start walking. Your footsteps echo. Behind you — another set of footsteps. You stop. They stop. You run. They run.
**Ending**: "Never be the last one in the hallway."

## 4.15 — The Basement Freezer
Mom sent you to get something from the basement freezer. The basement is dark. The freezer hums. You open it. Cold mist pours out. Among the frozen vegetables — a hand. You slam the lid. You run upstairs. Later, you tell yourself it was a frozen chicken. But chickens don't have fingers.
**Ending**: "Chickens don't have fingers."

## 4.16 — The Photograph That Changed
There's a family photo on the wall. Everyone is smiling. You've looked at it a thousand times. But tonight — something is different. Someone in the photo isn't smiling anymore. They're looking at you. You look away. Look back. Everyone is smiling again. But you know what you saw.
**Ending**: "Photos don't change. Do they?"

## 4.17 — The Knock at the Window
You're in bed. Second floor. There's a knock at the window. Three soft taps. You freeze. You tell yourself it's a tree branch. But there's no tree outside your window. The knock comes again. Three taps. You don't look. You pull the blanket over your head. The tapping continues. All night.
**Ending**: "Second floor. No tree."

## 4.18 — The Thing in the Backseat
You're in the car. Mom is driving. It's dark. You're in the backseat. You glance at the rearview mirror. The backseat is empty except for you. You look again. There's something in the seat next to you. You don't look. You don't move. You don't breathe. You stare at the back of Mom's head. You don't tell her. You don't want her to look in the mirror.
**Ending**: "Don't look in the backseat."

## 4.19 — The Shadow That Doesn't Match
You're walking home. The streetlights cast your shadow on the sidewalk. You stop. Your shadow keeps walking. Just for a second. Then it stops too. You wave. Your shadow waves — a moment too late. You run. Your shadow runs beside you. But it's not running the same way. Its legs are too long.
**Ending**: "Your shadow is supposed to follow you."

## 4.20 — The Breathing Outside the Door
You're in bed. The house is quiet. Then you hear it. Breathing. Slow. Heavy. Right outside your bedroom door. You stare at the crack under the door. The hallway light is on. Two shadows. Two feet. Standing right outside. You call out. "Mom?" The breathing stops. The feet don't move. The light stays on. The door stays closed. You don't call out again.
**Ending**: "Don't call out. Don't let it know you're awake."
